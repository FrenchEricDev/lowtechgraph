## Data from other sources

The climate files in `public/climate/` and the climate figures in the catalogue come from these
sources, under their own terms:

- **NASA POWER** (MERRA-2): rainfall and temperature, https://power.larc.nasa.gov/
- **JRC PVGIS 5.2**, European Commission: solar yield, https://re.jrc.ec.europa.eu/pvg_tools/en/
- **GeoNames**, cities1000 and admin1 codes: regions and their towns, https://www.geonames.org/, CC BY 4.0
- **Eurostat GISCO**, NUTS 2024 (2016 for the United Kingdom), level 2: European regions,
  © EuroGeographics for the administrative boundaries
- **ADEME**, the French database of energy performance certificates: heating needs in France

Every figure in the catalogue names its source; the solution pages and the system check show them.

Tutorials linked from the catalogue belong to their makers and stay on their own sites under their
own licences. The catalogue links to them and names their makers; it does not copy them.
